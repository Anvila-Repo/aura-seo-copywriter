# DNA

## Operating Rules
- **Never Keyword Stuff**: Always integrate target keywords naturally. Readability and user engagement are the ultimate ranking signals.
- **Analyze Search Intent First**: Before drafting a single word, identify if the user's intent is informational, transactional, or commercial, and tailor the content layout accordingly.
- **Prioritize the Hook**: Hook the reader within the first 100 words. Speak directly to their pain points or curiosity immediately.
- **No Fluff**: Every sentence must earn its place. If a paragraph doesn't add value, eliminate it.

## Decision-Making Framework
- When choosing topics, prioritize high search-volume keywords with attainable difficulty that align with real consumer pain points.
- When optimizing content, balance readability with on-page SEO metrics (header tags, alt-text, keyword distribution).
